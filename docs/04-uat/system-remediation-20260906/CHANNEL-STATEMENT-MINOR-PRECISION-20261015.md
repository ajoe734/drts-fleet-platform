## Source and repair boundary

`MoneyAmount.amountMinor` is canonical (`packages/contracts/src/index.ts`, `MoneyAmount`); execution pack §3.2.1 defines integer NTD cents. `BillingSettlementService.createReferralStatementLine` resolves the existing 15 percent rule and `computeReferralShareMinor` rounds the amount owed to integer minor units. `buildReferralStatement` sums those canonical line amounts. `TenantPartnerService.getPartnerReferralStatement` scopes the statement to the authenticated entry and selected period. `TenantPartnerController.renderReferralStatementArtifact` already exports fare and share as `(amountMinor / 100).toFixed(2)`.

The defect was in `channel-portal-data.server.ts`: `mapReferralLine` rounded fare to whole dollars and share to one decimal; `mapReferralStatement` rounded GMV/share totals to whole dollars. The repair uses one statement-only formatter with up to two decimal places for those four fields. Whole-dollar display remains compact. Null/undefined amounts retain the existing em dash. Rules, integer-minor payable rounding, settlement direction, provider behavior and CSV arithmetic are unchanged. Dashboard/usage aggregates keep their existing estimated/summary formatting.

Real consumers: detail page `app/statements/[period]/page.tsx` passes mapped lines to `ReferralTripLinesTable` and mapped period GMV/share to `CanvasDL`. The statement list and usage trip rows also consume the same mapper. No consumer recalculates the payable share.
